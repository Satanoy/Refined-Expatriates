
This mod adds an extra layer of complexity to the game's consumption and production of goods by integrating new, more complex production lines, 
several new consumer goods for each residential level, and adding vanilla products to the consumption items in residential levels to give more realism to the lives of your citizens.
It also integrates goods from some scenarios.

This mod is a very personal project that integrates, or attempts to integrate, everything I wanted to see in the game.

----------------------------------------
++++++++++VERY IMPORTANT!!!+++++++++++++
----------------------------------------
For this mod to work correctly, it must have all the game's DLC integrated. Because I suck at Blender, I've decided to use almost exclusively vanilla models from the game.
In addition to using many mechanics available ONLY in the different DLCs.
